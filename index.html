About Olive & Ember

Founded on summer nights and open flames, Olive & Ember brings a modern Mediterranean approach to seasonal cooking. We partner with local farms, sustainable fisheries and independent producers to craft dishes that are simple, vibrant and full of character.

  • Carefully curated wine list and cocktail program
  • Seasonal tasting menus and private dining
  • Outdoor seating and heated patio in cooler months
